## Why Now

I've always wanted a corner of my own — free from algorithms, free from judgments.
Here you'll find fragments of daily life, thoughts from work, and stray ideas that came out of nowhere.

## What This Site Will Hold

- **Journal**: book notes, films, travels, and bits that don't fit a category.
- **Tech Blog**: lessons from the trenches, things learned, experiments done.
- **Projects**: work I'm happy to show, and a reminder of what I've tried.

## To Close

Writing is a conversation with yourself. The speed comes from machines; the warmth comes from people.

> Slow and steady wins the race.
